Pickup-Mounted
Operator Cabin Concept

A concept design exploring how a pickup's cargo bed could accommodate an enclosed operator workspace. I developed a cabin layout bringing together seating, a computer workstation and air conditioning within the vehicle-mounted enclosure.

Cabin & workstation concept

Developed the cabin concept around the pickup's rear cargo area, considering how the enclosure, seating and computer workstation could be arranged together.

An air-conditioning unit was incorporated into the proposed layout. The drawing presents the relationship between the vehicle, cabin and interior workspace.

Concept presentation & confidentiality

This project represents a design concept for a mobile workspace, presented through a selected 2D drawing sheet.

The complete 3D model is not shared in this portfolio due to project confidentiality. The selected drawing communicates the concept and my layout-design contribution.
