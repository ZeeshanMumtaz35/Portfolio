Rail-Based Drone
Launch System

A mechanical launch assembly developed to support a drone's initial transition into flight. The system combines a supporting frame, guide rails and a pushing mechanism within an integrated mechanical assembly.

Project contribution

Our team developed the mechanical assembly, bringing together the frame, rail arrangement and pushing mechanism into a complete CAD model.

The project focused on the integration of these mechanical components as a drone launch system.

Launch concept

The drone is loaded from the rear of the assembly. A pushing mechanism moves it forward along the rails, providing initial motion before it leaves the launcher and transitions into flight.
