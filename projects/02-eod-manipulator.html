EOD Manipulator

Complete reverse engineering of an Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) manipulator. Our team recreated the mechanical assembly in CAD, representing the overall structure and the gripper used to handle suspicious objects from a distance.

Reverse engineering scope

Our work covered reverse engineering of the complete manipulator, recreating the mechanical design and component arrangement as an integrated assembly.

The project brings together the main structure, handling arm and gripper in a complete CAD representation.

Purpose & application

The manipulator is used in EOD operations to pick up and handle suspicious objects while allowing the operator to remain at a distance.

Its gripper provides the object-handling function during inspection work in operational areas.

02.1 / GRIPPER ASSEMBLY
Gripper & Object Handling

The front-end gripper holds objects so they can be picked up and lifted by the EOD manipulator. Sensors are incorporated into the gripper assembly as part of the overall system.

CAD assembly & fabrication

We developed the complete gripper CAD model and assembly as part of the reverse-engineering work. The project also included laser cutting of gripper components, connecting the digital model with physical fabrication.
