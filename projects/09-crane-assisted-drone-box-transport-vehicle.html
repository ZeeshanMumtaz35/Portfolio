Crane-Assisted
Drone Box Transport Vehicle

A reverse-engineered CAD vehicle concept combining a truck-mounted crane with a transport platform for drone storage boxes. Our team developed the digital assembly to illustrate how the boxes could be lifted and transferred between locations as part of a mobile handling concept.

Reverse-engineering contribution

Developed the vehicle model through reverse-engineering work, representing the truck, crane and storage-box arrangement within one CAD assembly.

The concept presents the crane and carrying platform together, showing the proposed vehicle layout for moving drone storage boxes.

Handling concept & project stage

The crane is intended to lift the storage boxes so they can be transferred from one position to another. The project explores this handling function at the vehicle-concept level.

This project remains a CAD model only. It has not been manufactured.
