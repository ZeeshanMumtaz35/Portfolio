Custom 41U
Vehicle Equipment Rack

A custom 41U equipment rack developed for a vehicle application. I completed the mechanical CAD design and assembly, working to standard rack requirements while adapting the enclosure and overall arrangement to the project's specific needs.

Image 1 of 2

Custom design contribution

Developed the rack's mechanical layout as a complete CAD assembly, bringing together the enclosure frame, vertical equipment-mounting rails and panel arrangement.

The work focused on a 41U rack tailored to the intended vehicle application, balancing the required rack format with a project-specific design.

Standard format, tailored assembly

Standard rack requirements guided the design, while the overall enclosure and component arrangement were customized for the project.
