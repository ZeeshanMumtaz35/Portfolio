Analysis Of Waste And Designing Of Lean Systems for Healthcare

Mapped the complete patient journey from arrival to discharge in a hospital Emergency Department using Value Stream Mapping (VSM). The project applied lean methods to analyze operational waste and improve the flow of care through the department.

Image 1 of 1

Method & contribution

Developed the current-state patient-flow map to understand the full process and identify opportunities for improvement. Applied VSM, 5S and Fishbone analysis to examine workflow issues and support the development of an improved lean system.

Prepared a comparison of the current and improved layouts, making the proposed process changes visible and connecting the analysis to a clearer patient-flow arrangement.

Outcome & academic relevance

The project contributed to reducing service time and operational waste in the Emergency Department through lean improvements to the patient-flow arrangement.

This final year project brings together my interests in Industrial Engineering, systems thinking and process optimization, complementing the mechanical design work presented elsewhere in the portfolio.
